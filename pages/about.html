About Beelinx

Fashion is everywhere. Finding it shouldn't be. Beelinx is a fashion discovery platform built for people who like finding new things. We bring clothing, brands and styles from different places into one space, instead of searching endlessly across Instagram, websites and different stores, you can simply explore. Whether you already know what you're looking for or you're just looking for something new, Beelinx is made to help you find it.

Why Beelinx?

Because sometimes you don't know what you're looking for yet. There are thousands of brands out there. Some you've heard of, most you haven't. Beelinx makes it easier to come across them. Scroll through different pieces, discover brands you haven't seen before, find styles that fit you, and follow the link when you find something you like. We're not here to tell you what to buy. We're here to help you find it.

WHAT WE'RE BUILDING

A better way to discover fashion. Fashion discovery shouldn't be limited to the brands the algorithm happens to put in front of you. We're building Beelinx to give smaller, independent and established brands another place to be discovered — while giving people a simpler way to explore what's out there. No pressure to buy. No endless searching. Just fashion worth discovering. Beelinx is where you find your next favourite brand.
